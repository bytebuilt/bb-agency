06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

No vague promises. Here's what clients usually want to know before we start.

How long does a website take? +

A Starter website usually takes 1–2 weeks, e-commerce 2–4 weeks, and custom builds 4+ weeks. Timing depends on scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

Who owns the website and code? +

You do. At handover, you receive ownership of the agreed deliverables, along with repository access and useful documentation where applicable.

Do you offer ongoing support? +

Yes. Maintenance options start from €30/month, depending on the updates and support you need.

Can you redesign my existing website? +

Absolutely. We can review what you have, identify what should stay or change, and plan the transition to minimise disruption.

How do payments work? +

Typically, 50% is paid to begin and 50% on delivery. Bank transfer, PayPal, or card can be discussed when we confirm the project.

Are hosting and domain included? +

They are quoted separately so you can see exactly what you are paying for. Third-party tools or payment provider fees may also apply.
